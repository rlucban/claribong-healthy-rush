📖 How to Play

🎮 Objective

Roll your ball through vibrant fruit-themed tracks! Collect healthy foods to boost your health and score, avoid junk food that damages you, and reach the finish line.

🕹️ Controls

🖥️ Desktop ← / → or A / D — Move Left/Right
Space / ↑ — Jump
📱 Mobile Drag finger on screen — Steer
Tap screen — Jump

🍎 Healthy Items (Collect These!)

🍓 Strawberry — Speed Boost 🍊 Orange — Health Restore 🍌 Banana — Combo Builder 🍉 Watermelon — Big Points 🍈 Papaya — Vitamin Bonus 🍋 Calamansi — Immunity Boost 🥕 Carrot — Eye Health 🥦 Broccoli — Super Food 🎃 Squash — Fiber Power 🍆 Eggplant — Brain Food 💧 Water — Hydration

🚫 Junk Food (AVOID!)

🍔 Burger — Heavy Damage 🍟 Fries — Grease Slow 🌭 Hotdog — Mystery Meat 🍩 Donut — Sugar Crash 🥤 Soda — Acid Burn 🍿 Chips — Empty Calories 💚 Slime — Toxic!

✨ Power-Ups

🧲 Magnet — Auto-collect 🛡️ Shield — Block 1 Hit
🌈 Super Mode: Collect 5 healthy items in a row without hitting junk to activate RAINBOW SUPER MODE! — Invincibility + max speed + bonus points!

🎯 Daily Goals

Complete each run's nutrition targets: 3 Fruits, 3 Veggies, 5 Waters. Track progress in the HUD!

🏆 Grading System

Earn up to 3 stars per run: ★ Survival (Level 5+), ★ Nutrition Goals met, ★ Mastery (5x combo or 75% purity). Check the Grading System screen for details!

📊 Grading System

How your Nutrition Report Card is calculated at the end of the run.

Your run is rated by 3 Star Objectives — earn stars for each goal you achieve!

  • ⭐ Star 1 - Completion/Survival
    Reached at least Level 5 (halfway) or crossed the finish line. No half-measures!
  • ⭐ Star 2 - Nutrition Goals
    Collected 3+ Fruits, 3+ Vegetables, and 5+ Water bottles in a single run.
  • ⭐ Star 3 - Mastery & Skill
    Achieved a 5x healthy combo streak or maintained 75%+ purity (healthy items vs total items).
Rating Results:
3 Stars — PERFECT RUN!
2 Stars — GREAT JOB!
1 Star — GOOD EFFORT!
0 Stars — TRY AGAIN!

Pro Tip: Build healthy combos, meet daily goals, and push through the full track for all 3 stars! 🏆
